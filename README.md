## 🎯 Objective

To perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on customer shopping data and uncover patterns in **purchasing behavior, preferences, and trends**.

---

## 📚 Project Overview

This project analyzes a dataset of **3900 customer transactions** with 18+ features, including demographics, purchase details, and behavioral attributes.

The goal is to extract meaningful insights related to:

- Customer demographics  
- Purchase patterns  
- Product preferences  
- Seasonal trends  
- Payment and discount behavior

## 📊 Key Insights

### 👥 Customer Demographics
- Average customer age: **~44 years**
- Majority fall under **Young Adult & Middle-Aged groups**

---

### 💰 Purchase Behavior
- Average purchase amount is **~$59–60 across categories**
- No major variation across product categories  

---

### 🛍️ Product Trends
- Most purchased categories:
  - Clothing  
  - Accessories  
- Popular items include:
  - Blouse, Pants, Jewelry, Sandals  

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Gender Insights
- Both genders show **similar purchasing behavior**
- No significant difference in spending  

---

### 📅 Seasonal Trends
- Purchases are **fairly evenly distributed across seasons**
- Slight increase in **Spring & Fall**

---

### ⭐ Customer Ratings
- Average rating across categories: **~3.7–3.8**
- Footwear received slightly higher ratings  

---

### 💳 Payment & Discounts
- Most used payment methods:
  - Debit Card  
  - Credit Card  
- Promo codes and discounts **do not significantly increase spending**

---

### 🔁 Customer Loyalty
- Average previous purchases: **~25**
- Indicates presence of repeat customers  

---

### 🚚 Shipping Preferences
- Standard, Free Shipping, and Express are commonly used  
- Preferences vary slightly across product categories  

---

### 🎨 Product Preferences
- Popular colors:
  - Olive, Yellow, Silver, Green  
- Wide distribution across color choices  

---

### 📊 Correlation Analysis
- Very weak correlation between:
  - Age and purchase amount  
  - Ratings and spending  
- Indicates **independent customer behavior factors**
